Design Thinking Process

Simple Clear Easy for Guidance

1.Empathize

I started by understanding the needs of my audience—potential clients and visitors interested in my media design work. I considered what they might look for: a clean layout, quick access to my portfolio, and an intuitive navigation experience.

2.Define

Based on this, I defined the problem as creating a website that minimizes clutter, highlights my work effectively, and provides clear guidance without overwhelming users.

3.Ideate

I brainstormed various layout options, focusing on a minimalistic design with a single-column structure. I decided to include a navigation bar with essential sections and a portfolio section to showcase my designs prominently.

4.Prototype

I created a basic version of the website with a beige background, a simple logo, and sections for portfolio items. I tested different font sizes and placements to ensure readability and accessibility.

5.Test

I sought feedback from a few peers, asking if they could easily find my work and navigate the site. Based on their input, I adjusted the spacing and refined the text to make it more concise, ensuring the website remained user-friendly.

6.Implement

I finalized the design by applying the feedback, optimizing performance, and ensuring the website aligns with my vision of simplicity and clarity.
