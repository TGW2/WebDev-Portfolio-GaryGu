Accessibility Statement

My Accessibility Commitment

I am committed to ensuring my website is accessible to everyone, including individuals with disabilities. My goal is to provide an inclusive digital experience that complies with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Planned Accessibility Features

  • Text Alternatives: I will provide descriptive alt text for all images and captions or transcripts for videos and audio content to support users with visual or hearing impairments.
  • Keyboard Navigation: I will ensure the website is fully navigable using a keyboard, enabling users with motor impairments to access all interactive elements.
  • High Color Contrast: I will use color schemes with a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 to enhance readability for users with low vision or color blindness.
  • Resizable Text: I will allow text to be resized up to 200% without loss of functionality or content, accommodating users who require larger text sizes.
  • Screen Reader Compatibility: I will use semantic HTML and ARIA landmarks to ensure compatibility with screen readers such as JAWS, NVDA, and VoiceOver.
  • Clear Forms and Instructions: I will provide descriptive labels for forms and clear error messages to assist users with cognitive or visual impairments.
  • Consistent Navigation: I will implement predictable navigation patterns to help users with cognitive disabilities navigate the site easily.
  • Focus Indicators: I will provide visible focus states for all interactive elements to support keyboard navigation users.
  • Skip Links: I will include a "skip to content" link to allow users to bypass repetitive navigation, improving efficiency for keyboard and screen reader users.

My Commitment to Accessibility

I plan to adhere to WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards and will continuously work to improve accessibility. I will regularly test my website using automated tools and manual reviews to identify and address potential barriers.

Known Limitations

As my website is still under development, some content or third-party integrations may not yet fully comply with WCAG standards. I plan to update these areas progressively and appreciate your patience as I enhance my site.

I will strive to respond to accessibility inquiries within 5 business days and can provide content in alternative formats upon request.

Ongoing Efforts

I plan to improve accessibility through training on inclusive design, regular audits, and user testing with individuals with disabilities. I aim to make my website a model of digital inclusivity.
